# Quillo: Product Sheet

*Fictional product for testing CALL-E. Brightfern Labs and Quillo are made up.*

## What is Quillo?

Quillo is an AI meeting assistant from Brightfern Labs. It joins your video
meetings, listens, and writes the notes for you, so everyone on the call can
focus on the conversation instead of typing. A few minutes after the meeting
ends, every attendee gets a clean summary with the key decisions and a list of
action items, each assigned to the person who agreed to do it.

Brightfern Labs was founded in 2021 and is based in Portland, Oregon. Quillo
launched publicly in 2023.

## Who it's for

Quillo is built for teams that live in meetings: sales teams running discovery
calls, customer success managers, recruiters doing back-to-back interviews, and
product teams with lots of internal syncs. It works best for teams of 5 to 200
people. Solo users can use the Free plan.

## Key features

**Automatic joining.** Connect your Google or Microsoft calendar once, and
Quillo joins every Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams meeting on it. You can
switch it off for any individual meeting.

**Summaries in about two minutes.** After the call, Quillo emails a summary with
decisions, open questions, and action items. Summaries are also posted to a
Slack channel of your choice.

**Searchable archive.** Every transcript is stored and searchable. Type a
keyword like "pricing" or "renewal" and jump to the exact moment it was said.

**Speaker labels.** Quillo identifies who said what, so action items are
assigned to the right person.

**CRM sync (Team plan).** Notes and action items are pushed to HubSpot
automatically after sales calls, attached to the right contact and deal.

**Languages.** Quillo transcribes English, Spanish, French, and German.

## Privacy

Recordings and transcripts are encrypted. Meeting hosts can delete any
recording at any time, and workspace admins can set an automatic deletion
period. Quillo shows a short notice in the meeting chat so attendees know the
meeting is being recorded.

## Getting started

Sign up at the Brightfern Labs website with a Google or Microsoft account,
connect your calendar, and Quillo will join your next meeting. Setup takes
about five minutes.
